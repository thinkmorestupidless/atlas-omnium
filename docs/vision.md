# Vision

Atlas Omnium is an explorable, connected representation of physical reality as humanity
currently understands it. The user encounters a thing, explores where it is, what surrounds it,
what it belongs to and what it is made of, and follows those relationships without losing the
sense that everything belongs to one reality. The limits of what has been observed are part of the
atlas, not a gap it papers over.

## Principles

1. **Reality first.** Real identities, relationships and evidence over spectacle. No invented
   planets, surfaces or measurements.
2. **The universe is the interface.** Selecting and navigating things is primary; panels, menus
   and search are secondary.
3. **Continuous context, changing representation.** A selected object keeps its identity across
   scales even as the rendering switches from orbital geometry to points or fields.
4. **Every object is a doorway.** Surroundings, containing systems, constituents, movement, history
   and evidence, where available.
5. **Show the limits of knowledge.** Observed, calculated, inferred, estimated, reconstructed and
   illustrative are different things and look different.
6. **Time matters.** A coordinate without its frame and epoch is not a coordinate.
7. **Different physics deserves different graphics.** No single camera metaphor from galaxies to
   atoms; a molecule is not a tiny solar system.
8. **Beauty through restraint.** Geometric marks, typography, deliberate colour, elegant movement.
   No obligatory black starfield, no photorealistic texture.
9. **Traceable claims.** A displayed fact resolves to a source record, release and transformation,
   or abstains.
10. **Incremental coverage.** A coherent curated slice first; independently validated domains
    added over time.

## Four navigation questions

| Mode | Question | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial | Where is it relative to something else? | positions, frames, distance, epoch |
| Scale | What becomes visible at another order of magnitude? | a change of projection and mark type |
| Structural | What is it part of, what is it made of? | typed part-of relationships; a type or specimen is not a tracked individual |
| Temporal | What changes over time? | a valid temporal model per domain |

The product does not collapse these into a camera zoom. Scale travel is continuous within a
spatial regime; a structural step is an explicit jump the interface announces.

## Two kinds of connection

Physical relations (orbits, gravitation, containment, position, composition) and knowledge
relations (observed by, derived from, same as a catalogue entry, supported by, superseded by) are
different edges with different marks. Neither is ever drawn in the style of a graphical line such
as a label's leader.

## The first slice: 001 earth

One screen that explores real Solar System objects under real time-dependent positions, transitions
into a small true-data stellar neighbourhood while the Sun keeps its identity, and demonstrates one
explicitly structural inward path from Earth to water to its atoms. The journey, the acceptance
criteria and the measures are in `evaluation.md`. Success is not how much of the universe is shown;
it is whether this small, real, connected part makes the larger vision believable.

If time runs short, the outward astronomical journey and data fidelity are protected and the
inward branch is deferred, never shortcut into something misleading.

## Audience

Curious people who want to explore physical reality through interaction: technically minded
adults, educators, students, astronomy enthusiasts. No astronomy background is required to use it;
experts find the source details, units, assumptions and uncertainty one affordance away.

## Later, deliberately provisional

Richer astronomy (exoplanets, more of the neighbourhood, galaxies, catalogue identity
reconciliation); terrestrial continuity inward and outward; large-scale cosmology from its own
coordinate assumptions; a living atlas with versioned refreshes and contributor workflows. None
starts at full scale before the previous stage has a reliable data and visual model.
