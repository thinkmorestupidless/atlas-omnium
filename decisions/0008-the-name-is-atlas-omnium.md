# 0008: The product is called Atlas Omnium

*2026-10-23.* Accepted.

## Decision
The product's name is **Atlas Omnium.**, with its full stop, replacing "an atlas of everything.".
The repository is `thinkmorestupidless/atlas-omnium` on GitHub, public. The promise is unchanged:
"Every place. Every scale. Everything we know."

## Why
Trevor chose the name. It is Latin for an atlas of all things, so the meaning stays; and it works
as a proper name, where the old one read as a description.

## Alternatives
Keep "an atlas of everything.", as the brief had it.

## Consequences
The lock-up, the page title, the README, the package, the site membership file and the ingest's
user agent carry the new name. The brief is renamed `atlas-omnium-product-spec.md` and keeps its
text, with a note of the change. Local checkout directories may keep the old name; nothing reads it.
